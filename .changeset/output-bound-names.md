michi-core: Breaking: the NAPI byte budget now charges a name for every place a render writes it, so the 16 MiB cap bounds the output as it said it did. A `truncated` row repeats the cell's field name, and the section name when two or more sections are cut, for every cut cell, and the `totalCount` table repeats the section name, but each was charged once. A 4 KiB field name with 1,000 rows of one 201-char cell held 261 KB and rendered to 95 times that in text and 112 times in JSON, and at 60,000 rows `renderText()` failed with `Failed to convert rust String into napi string`. In JavaScript, `AgentResponse.items`, `partialSuccess`, `pipeline`, `status` and `renderToon` now charge a field name (and a section name) once for each string cell over 200 chars, `partialSuccess` and `pipeline` charge their section name twice (its header and its `totalCount` row), and `hint` and `recoveryHint` charge 1 and 4 bytes more than their text. A call or response past 16 MiB throws `input byte total N exceeds maximum of 16777216` or `cumulative byte total N exceeds maximum of 16777216`, where that hostile 60,000-row table was accepted. Normal responses (short names, cells under 200 chars) are charged almost the same as before. A render is at most 19 times the bytes held plus 4 KiB on every surface, and tests measure it over a matrix of hostile responses.
